Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 3, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Liora collects only what is needed to provide its service. When you create an account we store an anonymous identifier and your selected preferences (prayer times, denomination). If you sign in with Apple, your email address is stored by our authentication provider (Firebase) solely for account recovery — we do not use it for marketing or share it with third parties. Your payment details are handled directly by Apple and never seen by us.

2. Health Data

With your permission, Liora reads heart rate and heart-rate variability (HRV) from Apple Health to detect signs of stress and offer gentle prayer reminders. This data is processed entirely on your device. It is never uploaded to our servers, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

3. Location Data

If you use the Church Finder feature, Liora accesses your location only while the app is open and only to find nearby places of worship. Your location is not stored or shared.

4. Advertising

Free users may see ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect advertising identifiers and usage data in accordance with Google's privacy policy. You can opt out of personalised ads at any time in your device settings. Premium subscribers see no ads.

5. Analytics

We use Firebase (Google) for anonymous crash reporting and performance monitoring. No personally identifiable information is linked to these reports.

6. Data Retention

Your profile, profile photo (if uploaded), journal entries, prayer intentions, and game progress are stored in Firebase Firestore linked to your account. You may delete your account at any time by going to Profile → Delete Account. Your Firestore data is removed immediately; any remaining authentication record is purged within 30 days.

7. Children

Liora is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance. The date at the top of this page always reflects the latest revision.
